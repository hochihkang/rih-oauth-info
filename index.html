ResearchIntelligenceHub

A personal tool for keeping up with new papers and research developments. It organizes research alerts I subscribe to, removes repeated article entries, and makes titles, links and short excerpts easier to read and find.

The tool uses Gmail read-only access (gmail.readonly). It selects alerts by labels and dates; the permission itself allows broader mailbox access. It does not send, delete or modify email.

For my own use only. My mailbox contents and reading records are not displayed on this website.
