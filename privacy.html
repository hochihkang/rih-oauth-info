Privacy Policy

This personal reading tool uses Gmail read-only access to read selected research alerts and associated metadata. It keeps article titles, links, short excerpts and dates for reading and deduplication. The permission allows broader mailbox access; the program filters which messages it processes.

Records are kept in private storage. Data can be processed locally or by hosting and AI services authorized for these reading functions. Selected article details can be sent to a destination when I request it. Mailbox contents and reading records are not published on this website.

Google user data is used only for these reading functions. It must not be sold, used for advertising, or used to develop, improve or train non-personalized AI models. Its use and transfer will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use, and the Google Workspace User Data Policy.

I can revoke this app's Gmail access in my Google Account and manage deletion of retained records and backups. Revocation does not itself delete existing copies. Separately authorized services have their own access and retention controls.

For access or data questions, use the support contact shown on Google's authorization screen. These pages have no forms or analytics scripts; the host may keep ordinary access logs.
